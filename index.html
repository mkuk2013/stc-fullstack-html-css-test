⚠ Examination Policy

Strict Test Rules

These rules apply to every student. Any violation will automatically cancel the test — no second warning will be given.

🔁 One-Time Attempt

Each student can take the test ONLY ONCE. After one start / submit / cancel, the system automatically blocks any further attempt.

🚫 Tab / Page Change = Cancel

During the test, switching tabs, minimizing the browser, or opening another app/page will immediately CANCEL the test.

❌ Page Close / Refresh = Cancel

Closing or refreshing the page will also cancel the test, and your attempt will be recorded.

🖥 Full-Screen Mode

The entire test runs in full-screen mode. Exiting full-screen will also cause cancellation.

⏱ 100 Minutes Fixed Time

A total of 100 minutes for 100 questions. When time ends, the test submits automatically — no extra time is given.

📵 No Copy / Paste

Copy, paste and right-click are disabled during the test. Think and answer on your own — this test measures your real progress.
